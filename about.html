Purpose & Direction

Guided By Craft, Rooted In Permanence

01 • Our Mission

To Replace Disposable Decor With Generational Heirlooms

We exist to slow down the furniture industry. Every table, sofa, and luminaire we create is engineered from kiln-dried solid hardwoods, traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery, and zero-VOC organic oils so your home feels grounded, healthy, and timeless.

100% Solid FSC-Certified Timber Zero Particleboard
02 • Our Vision

To Pioneer Climate-Positive Architectural Living Worldwide

We envision a future where luxury interiors actively regenerate forests and preserve endangered woodworking guilds. By 2030, every FurniCraft showroom will operate on carbon-neutral energy while funding apprenticeship ateliers for next-generation artisans.

3 Trees Replanted Per Piece Lifetime Refinishing Program
Our History & Evolution

A steady, deliberate journey from a two-bench timber barn to an internationally recognized furniture and home decor studio.

2010 Chapter 01

The Timber Barn

Material Provenance

Traceable Elements From Forest To Living Room

We source only raw materials that develop a richer patina with every passing decade.

Timber • North America & Burma

Kiln-Dried Teak & Black Walnut

Seasoned for 18 months to 8% moisture content to prevent warping, then hand-rubbed with pure cold-pressed linseed oil and beeswax.
