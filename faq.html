Expert Answers & Support

Section 01

White-Glove Delivery & Transit Questions

Everything you need to know about how your heirloom furniture travels from our woodworking atelier to your living room.

Q1 Is white-glove assembly included with every order?

Yes! All orders over $1,500 include complimentary white-glove delivery, room-of-choice placement, professional hardware leveling, and 100% recycling of all eco-packaging materials.

Q2 What is the typical lead time for custom wood furniture?

Because each solid teak or walnut table is hand-planed and finished to order by our guild artisans, standard manufacturing and delivery lead times range between 3 to 5 weeks.

Section 02

Solid Wood Warranty & Maintenance

Learn how to preserve the natural patina and structural integrity of your kiln-dried furniture.

Q3 What does your 10-year solid wood warranty cover?

Our 10-year structural warranty covers any defects in mortise-and-tenon joinery, frame splitting, or structural warping resulting from standard residential usage and climate seasoning.

Q4 How often should I condition my wooden dining table?

We recommend applying our complimentary organic beeswax and walnut oil balm once every 6 months to nourish the heartwood and maintain its water-repellent satin finish.

Section 03

Showroom Appointments & 3D Planning

Details on scheduling your visit to our flagship design district atrium in Suite 100.

Q5 Are walk-ins welcome at the flagship showroom?

Yes, walk-ins are always welcome during our daily operating hours (10 AM – 8:30 PM). However, booking an appointment in advance guarantees a dedicated senior architect and espresso bar priority.

Q6 How does the $250 showroom styling credit work?

When you book a private showroom consultation, your account receives an automatic $250 credit that is applied directly toward your first custom furniture or decor purchase.

Section 04

Custom Orders & Performance Fabrics

Guidance on selecting stain-resistant weaves and adjusting table and sectional dimensions.

Q7 Are your performance fabrics pet and child friendly?

Absolutely. Our Belgian bouclé and raw linen weaves are tightly loomed without chemical coatings, offering exceptional snag resistance and effortless spill blotting for busy households.

Q8 Can I request custom dimensions for sectionals?

Yes, our modular sectionals and dining tables can be adjusted in length and depth down to the exact inch to suit your architectural alcoves and room proportions.

Section 05

Trade Accounts & 0% Financing Terms

Information for interior designers, commercial architects, and retail financing applicants.

Q9 How do I qualify for the 20% interior designer trade tier?

Simply submit your professional resale certificate, design license, or EIN number through our trade portal on the contact page for instant 20% wholesale pricing approval.

Q10 Is 0% APR financing available for online orders?

Yes, qualifying purchases over $2,000 are eligible for 0% APR financing spread across 12, 18, or 24 monthly installments with no hidden prepayment penalties.

Section 06 • Sustainability

How Are FurniCraft Materials Verified As Sustainable?

Every log and textile batch we source carries strict chain-of-custody certification from the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC®) and OEKO-TEX®. We ensure zero deforestation and complete transparency from forest floor to final furniture assembly.

3 Saplings Planted Per Order

Your purchase directly funds community reforestation projects in protected tropical and temperate reserves.

Section 07 • Craftsmanship

What Happens During The 4-Week Atelier Build?

Once your custom order is placed, our guild master joiners hand-select raw timber slabs, air-dry them to precise humidity specs, hand-carve interlocking joinery, and apply three coats of organic plant-based oils.
